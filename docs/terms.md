# Care Circle terms of use (hackathon prototype)

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

1. **Not an emergency service.** Care Circle does not watch over anyone, detect falls or contact emergency services. In an emergency, call 911. For thoughts of self-harm, call or text 988.
2. **Not medical advice.** Care Circle does not give medical, dosing or drug advice. It repeats only the medicine names and times your family entered. Consult a healthcare provider for personal medical decisions.
3. **A logged dose is a statement, not proof.** Care Circle records what someone says. It cannot confirm that a medicine was actually taken.
4. **Who it suits.** Care Circle suits people who can reliably report on themselves. It isn't suitable for people with moderate or advanced dementia.
5. **Separate accounts.** Each person links their own account. If two people share one account, Care Circle can't tell them apart.
6. **Prototype.** This software is provided "as is" under the MIT License, without warranty. Emails can be delayed or fail; don't rely on Care Circle as the only way you stay in touch.
